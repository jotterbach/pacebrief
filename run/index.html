      Your runner profiles, courses and settings are stored only in this browser's storage on this device (its database, IndexedDB, and local storage). They are never uploaded to us, and your course file is read right here in the browser. Weather lookups send only the coordinates of points on and around the course and the start date to Open-Meteo; map and terrain tiles for the area you view come from OpenFreeMap and AWS.

      Clearing site data, cookies or the browser cache deletes them, and in a private window they are gone when you close it. Export a backup file to keep them or to move them to another device.
