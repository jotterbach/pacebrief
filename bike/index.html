    The plan switches position by terrain: race position on fast flat stretches, the hoods on climbs, the drops on steep descents. Each position changes the drag (CdA) and always starts a new section.

    For a past date you get the actual weather at that hour; for the next ~16 days, the forecast. Further ahead, enter typical values. Empty fields use standard air (15 °C, 1013 hPa, no wind).

    The plan condensed to a strip for the stem or top tube: each climb its own row. Print, PDF, SVG or PNG.

    Your rider profiles, courses and settings are stored only in this browser's storage on this device (its database, IndexedDB, and local storage). They are never uploaded to us, and your course file is read right here in the browser. Weather lookups send only the coordinates of points on and around the course and the start date to Open-Meteo; map and terrain tiles for the area you view come from OpenFreeMap and AWS.

    Clearing site data, cookies or the browser cache deletes them, and in a private window they are gone when you close it. Export a backup file to keep them or to move them to another device.
